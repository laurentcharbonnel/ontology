# Enterprise Ontology

A working knowledge base for designing enterprise ontologies and connecting business semantics to implementable data models.

## Purpose
Establish a shared business vocabulary, explicit concepts and relationships, and traceability from business meaning to conceptual, logical and physical data models.

## Contents
- [Ontology framing](docs/ontology-framing.md): scope, terminology, standards and workflow
- [Model transformation](docs/model-transformation.md): ontology to conceptual, logical and physical models
- [Architecture questions](docs/architecture-questions.md): integration, ownership and next decisions

## Guiding principles
1. Business meaning comes before storage structures.
2. Keep vocabulary, ontology, data models and implementation artifacts distinct but linked.
3. Make assumptions, provenance and modeling decisions explicit.
4. Introduce formal semantics only where they provide measurable value.
